# Illustrated meat-card culinary notes

Reviewed 2026-10-08. The 70 cards use the researched bilingual descriptions as their main description, replacing the original location sentences. Each replacement has its own reviewed Marin recording.

Descriptions are tendencies, not promises: breed, age, fatness, trimming and cooking change the result. Browning aroma is described as a cooking effect rather than an inherent aroma of every raw cut. General cooking suggestions are editorial synthesis from the cut's fat, grain and connective tissue.

Primary references:

- [MAFF: beef, pork and chicken cuts](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/pr/aff/2009/spe1_02.html): anatomical position, general character, offal and common uses.
- [Japan Meat Information Service: cooking tougher cuts](https://www4.jmi.or.jp/recipe/cooking/niru_main3.html): connective tissue and moist cooking.
- [NH Foods: beef cuts](https://www.nipponham.co.jp/recipes/meat/knowledge/beaf/): main beef cuts, grain, fat and cooking applications.
- [NH Foods: pork cuts](https://www.nipponham.co.jp/recipes/meat/knowledge/pork/): shoulder, loin, tenderloin, belly and leg characteristics.
- [NH Foods: pork cooking uses](https://www.nipponham.co.jp/recipes/meat/knowhow/pork/01.html): matching cuts to cooking methods.
- [NH Foods: chicken cuts](https://www.nipponham.co.jp/recipes/meat/knowledge/poultry/): breast, thigh, tenderloin, skin and wings.
- [Japan Meat Distribution Center: pork cuts](https://www.piif.jmtc.or.jp/butabui/): standard retail cuts and anatomy.
- [Japan Meat Information Service: kurashita](https://www.jmi.or.jp/info/word/ka/ka_097.html): shoulder-loin regional name, explicitly described with beef. The pork diagram label is not treated as a separately verified standard pork cut.
- [MAFF yakitori publication](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/pr/aff/attach/pdf/download-3.pdf): chicken offal and yakitori textures.
- [FAMIC chicken-part Q&A](https://www.famic.go.jp/public_relations_magazine/kouhoushi/back_number/202601_81.pdf): chicken parts and rare-cut terminology.
- [Daikokudo yakitori specialist: cuts](https://yakitori-ya.com/html/page2.html): rare chicken cuts, organs and culinary textures.
- [J・S restaurant operator: tsunagi/hatsumoto](https://js-dining.jp/column/tsunagi-yakitori/): overlapping commercial names, vessels near heart and characteristic chew.
- [J・S restaurant operator: yakiniku cuts](https://js-dining.jp/column/yakiniku-bui-ichiran/): rare beef cuts, retail/menu terminology.
- [Yagiya restaurant menu](https://yakiniku-yagiya.com/pdf/menu_240425.pdf): kainomi and individual knuckle muscles.

Naming qualifications: pork 腰 is a region rather than a standard retail cut; 鞍下, 外バラ, 中バラ, 前バラ and 肩バラ cannot be assigned a unique universal pork-cut specification from this illustration. カルビ is a menu category, not one muscle. Chicken ハラミ is abdominal muscle, not a mammalian diaphragm. はつもと/つなぎ can overlap and trimming varies by shop. White-liver colour alone does not establish quality.
