The existing てしまう and ておく formation fields now show the corresponding ちゃう／じゃう and とく／どく contractions. Related forms such as といて and negative-regret なきゃよかった receive their own contextual exercises.

New colloquial expressions use a natural polite equivalent in the Formal / polite example; the equivalent is not necessarily a word-for-word contraction. The formation and English explanation state the distinction. Each drill question has one answer matching its meaning and register cue.

References: [Tae Kim’s original casual patterns notes](https://guidetojapanese.org/learn/grammar/slang), [Japan Foundation’s intermediate contents](https://marugoto.jpf.go.jp/assets/docs/about/intermediate2_contents.pdf).

New Nova drill prompts and Japanese explanations use generation speed 1.0; answer fragments and choice lists use 0.85. 文法カード keeps the requested Marin 1.0 for headings and examples. Exact reviewed inputs are saved in each lesson review file. No audible listening/pitch verification is claimed.
