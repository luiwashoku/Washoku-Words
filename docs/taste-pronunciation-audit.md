## Findings

### 1. One definite input-reading correction

**Card 29 — 絹ごし豆腐:** `きぬごしとうふ` → `きぬごしどうふ`. The source annotation uses 豆腐(とうふ) inside this compound. The dictionary compound is きぬごしどうふ. The proposed correction is recorded below but has not been applied to either display or audio.

[Dictionary: デジタル大辞泉 / 日本国語大辞典](https://kotobank.jp/word/絹漉し豆腐-475112)

### 2. Particle は: listen before regenerating

153 entries (150 unique clips) contain particle は without an explicit わ cue. These are listening candidates, **not confirmed mispronunciations**. The 10 最初は sentences already have the cue and need no further regeneration based on text alone.

The JSON records each reviewed particle position and a proposed speech input. It preserves lexical ha, including ごはん, はくさい, はまぐり, はちみつ, はっきり, こはだ, and 歯ごたえ. No directional particle へ occurs in this deck. The conventional particle を remains を.

### 3. Two food names currently share one recording

**Card 62 牡蠣 and card 170 柿** both map to `かき` and therefore the same MP3. Their standard-Japanese pitch accents differ. Hiragana alone cannot represent that distinction. Listen to the shared clip before deciding which needs replacement; future audio lookup for these names should distinguish the card/meaning as well as the reading.

[Accent distinction: 三省堂 新明解日本語アクセント辞典 introduction](https://dictionary.sanseido-publ.co.jp/wordpress/wp-content/themes/dictionaryandbeyond/pdf/ssd13672.pdf)

The other shared name, ごま豆腐 / 胡麻豆腐, is the same food name and is safe to reuse.
